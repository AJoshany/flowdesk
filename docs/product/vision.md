# FlowDesk — Product Vision

## 1. Product Overview

FlowDesk is a modern CRM dashboard application designed to help small
and growing teams manage their customer relationships, sales pipeline,
activities, and team members from a centralized workspace.

The product provides a structured workspace where teams can manage
customers, track deals, monitor activities, and collaborate with team
members.

---

## 2. Problem

Small teams often manage customer relationships across multiple
disconnected tools such as spreadsheets, messaging applications, and
task management platforms.

This makes it difficult to:

- Maintain a reliable source of customer information
- Track the status of sales opportunities
- Understand the current sales pipeline
- Keep track of customer-related activities
- Manage team responsibilities and permissions

FlowDesk aims to provide a centralized and structured solution for
these problems.

---

## 3. Target Users

FlowDesk is primarily designed for small and growing teams that need
a lightweight CRM system.

The primary user roles are:

- Owner
- Manager
- Member

Each role may have different permissions and responsibilities within
a workspace.

---

## 4. Core Product Areas

The initial product is centered around the following areas:

### Authentication

Users can securely register, sign in, sign out, and access their
workspace.

### Workspace

A workspace represents a team or organization using FlowDesk.

### Dashboard

The dashboard provides an overview of important CRM information,
including sales and customer-related metrics.

### Customers

Users can manage customer records and access customer-related
information.

### Deals

Users can create and manage sales opportunities and track their
progress through the sales pipeline.

### Activities

Users can track activities related to customers and deals.

### Team

Workspace members can be managed according to their roles and
permissions.

---

## 5. Product Goal

The primary goal of FlowDesk is to provide a simple, structured, and
efficient CRM experience for small teams.

The product should make it easy for users to:

- Understand the current state of their business
- Manage customer relationships
- Track sales opportunities
- Organize customer-related activities
- Collaborate with their team
- Control access through roles and permissions

---

## 6. MVP Scope

The initial MVP will focus on the following capabilities:

- User authentication
- Workspace creation
- Role-based access control
- Dashboard
- Customer management
- Deal management
- Activity management
- Team management

Features outside of this scope should not be implemented unless they
are explicitly added to the product requirements.

---

## 7. Product Principles

### Simplicity

The product should remain easy to understand and use.

### Consistency

User interactions, UI patterns, terminology, and behavior should be
consistent throughout the application.

### Security

Authentication, authorization, and user data must be handled securely.

### Maintainability

The system should be structured so that features can evolve without
creating unnecessary coupling or complexity.

### Scalability

The architecture should allow the product to grow beyond the initial
MVP without requiring a complete rewrite.

---

## 8. Out of Scope

The following areas are outside the initial MVP unless explicitly
introduced later:

- Marketing automation
- Email marketing campaigns
- Advanced reporting
- AI-powered CRM features
- Mobile applications
- Billing and subscription management
- Third-party CRM integrations
- Advanced workflow automation

---

## 9. Definition of Product Success

The MVP is successful when a small team can:

1. Create and access a workspace.
2. Manage its members and permissions.
3. Add and manage customers.
4. Create and track deals.
5. Record customer-related activities.
6. Use the dashboard to understand the current state of the CRM.
